# Taxonomy

## Categories

| Category | Definition | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Billing › Refund request | The customer asks for money back for a past charge | "I want a refund for last month" |
| Billing › Wrong charge | A charge the customer did not expect or authorise | "I was charged twice" |
| Classes › Schedule | Class times, time zones, recordings | "The classes are at times I can't make" |
| Account › Access | Logging in, email changes, lockouts | "I can't log in" |
| Other | Nothing else fits | — |

## Priorities

- **P1:** the customer cannot use the product at all, or is losing money or data now, with no workaround.
- **P2:** a paid feature is broken for this customer, or a charge is wrong.
- **P3:** a request or question about billing, plans or classes, with nothing broken.
- **P4:** feedback or a question with no time pressure.
