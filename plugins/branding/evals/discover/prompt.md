The current look — a green leaf logo and a generic sans-serif — makes us look like every other eco brand. Our customers are mostly 30-45-year-old renters in cities who care about plastic waste but hate faff. Competitors include Smol, Ocean Saver and Splosh. Before we brief a designer, I want a clear picture of where our brand stands today, how those competitors present themselves, who our audience is and where the gaps are. Call the project `fernway` and save the brief. I won't be around to answer questions, so go with what's here and write down any assumptions you make.
