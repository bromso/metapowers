## Purpose

The Cash Flow Stress utility models liquidity risk by simulating adverse cash flow scenarios — delayed collections, accelerated payments, revenue shortfalls — to determine cash runway and identify potential liquidity crises.

## What It Does

<Steps>
<Step>Models baseline cash flow projections</Step>
<Step>Applies stress scenarios (revenue drop, collection delays, cost spikes)</Step>
<Step>Calculates cash runway under each stress scenario</Step>
<Step>Identifies the point at which cash reserves are exhausted</Step>
<Step>Recommends liquidity buffers and contingency measures</Step>
</Steps>

## Output

<Output>
- Stressed cash flow projections by scenario
- Cash runway analysis (months of coverage)
- Liquidity risk assessment
- Recommended cash reserve levels
</Output>
