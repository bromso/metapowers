## Purpose

The Ratios utility calculates standard financial ratios to assess the organization's liquidity, profitability, efficiency, and leverage, providing a snapshot of financial health.

## What It Does

<Steps>
<Step>Calculates liquidity ratios (current ratio, quick ratio, cash ratio)</Step>
<Step>Computes profitability ratios (gross margin, operating margin, ROE, ROA)</Step>
<Step>Analyzes efficiency ratios (asset turnover, inventory turnover, DSO)</Step>
<Step>Evaluates leverage ratios (debt-to-equity, interest coverage, debt-to-assets)</Step>
<Step>Benchmarks against industry standards where available</Step>
</Steps>

## Output

<Output>
- Ratio dashboard organized by category
- Period-over-period trend analysis
- Industry benchmark comparison
- Flagged ratios outside healthy ranges
</Output>
