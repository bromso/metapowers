## Purpose

The Unit Economics utility calculates per-unit financial metrics — customer acquisition cost (CAC), lifetime value (LTV), gross margin, contribution margin — to evaluate the fundamental economics of the business model.

## What It Does

<Steps>
<Step>Calculates customer acquisition cost (CAC) by channel</Step>
<Step>Models customer lifetime value (LTV) using retention and revenue data</Step>
<Step>Computes LTV:CAC ratio and payback period</Step>
<Step>Analyzes gross and contribution margins by product or segment</Step>
<Step>Identifies unit economics breakeven points</Step>
</Steps>

## Output

<Output>
- CAC by acquisition channel
- LTV with assumptions (retention rate, ARPU, margin)
- LTV:CAC ratio and payback period
- Margin analysis by product or segment
</Output>
