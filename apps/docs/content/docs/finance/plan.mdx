## Purpose

The Plan phase translates business strategy into financial objectives, defining measurable targets and key performance indicators that guide forecasting and budgeting.

## Prerequisites

The financial assessment must exist at `.singularskills/finance/<project>/01-assess.md`.

## What It Does

<Steps>
<Step>Reviews business strategy and growth objectives</Step>
<Step>Defines financial targets (revenue, margin, growth rate)</Step>
<Step>Establishes KPIs for monitoring progress</Step>
<Step>Sets planning assumptions (market growth, pricing, headcount)</Step>
<Step>Writes the strategic financial plan to `.singularskills/finance/<project>/02-plan.md`</Step>
</Steps>

## Output

<Output>
Creates `.singularskills/finance/<project>/02-plan.md` containing:

- Strategic financial objectives with targets
- KPI definitions and measurement criteria
- Planning assumptions and dependencies
- Timeline and milestones
</Output>
