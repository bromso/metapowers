## Purpose

The Verbal Identity phase brings the brand personality to life in words. It creates the voice, tone, messaging framework, and language guidelines that ensure consistent communication.

## Usage

**Example:**
```
/branding:verbal acme
```

## Prerequisites

Requires `.metapowers/branding/<brand>/02-strategy.md` from the Strategy phase. Use `--skip-checks` to bypass.

## What It Does

<Steps>
<Step>Defines brand voice based on personality traits ("We are / We are not")</Step>
<Step>Creates tone variations for different contexts (marketing, support, social, formal)</Step>
<Step>Develops 3-5 messaging pillars with proof points</Step>
<Step>Generates tagline candidates and recommends the strongest</Step>
<Step>Establishes language guidelines (preferred vocabulary, words to avoid, style)</Step>
<Step>Writes the verbal identity to `.metapowers/branding/<brand>/03-verbal.md`</Step>
</Steps>

## Output

<Output>
Creates `.metapowers/branding/<brand>/03-verbal.md` containing:

- Voice description and examples
- Tone variations by context
- Messaging pillars with headlines and proof points
- Tagline recommendation
- Language do's and don'ts
</Output>
