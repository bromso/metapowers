## Purpose

The Investigate skill explains a metric's move in an order that catches broken data before it gets explained.

## Usage

**Example:**
```
/analytics-investigate <metric and change>
```

## Prerequisites

The metric's series and the rows or segment totals behind it.

## What It Does

<Steps>
<Step>Sizes the change against its normal range</Step>
<Step>Rules out tracking changes, late data and definition changes</Step>
<Step>Decomposes the change by segment and into mix versus rate</Step>
<Step>Ranks candidate causes with evidence for and against</Step>
<Step>Says what would confirm the top cause</Step>
</Steps>

## Output

<Output>
Creates `.singularskills/analytics/<project>/investigations/<date>-<slug>.md`.
</Output>
