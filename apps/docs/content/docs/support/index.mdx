The Support domain sets up a customer support operation in seven phases: map, taxonomy, knowledge, responses, escalation, service levels and health. Each phase writes a numbered file to `.singularskills/support/<project>/` with the decisions made and the questions still open. Three utilities answer one ticket, review one conversation, and draft customer updates during an outage, reading whatever the phases have written.

Every number a support skill reports is labelled `computed` (from data you supplied, with the script shown), `not measured` or `target`. Ticket quotes in artifacts have names, emails, phone numbers and account numbers replaced by placeholders.

## Support Phases

| Phase | Command | Purpose |
|-------|---------|---------|
| Map | `/support-journey-map` | Channels, journey moments, contact reasons and volumes |
| Taxonomy | `/support-taxonomy` | Categories, tags and P1–P4 priorities, tested on real tickets |
| Knowledge | `/support-knowledge-base` | Help centre structure, ranked article backlog, top three articles |
| Responses | `/support-macros` | Saved replies in the brand's voice, and the support policy |
| Escalation | `/support-escalation` | Tiers, triggers and handoffs to other teams |
| Service levels | `/support-service-levels` | Response and resolution targets and the staffing they need |
| Health | `/support-health-review` | Metrics, SLA attainment and accounts at risk |

## Utility Skills

| Skill | Command | Purpose |
|-------|---------|---------|
| Reply | `/support-reply` | Draft a reply to one ticket, checked against policy |
| QA Review | `/support-qa-review` | Score one conversation with quoted evidence |
| Incident Update | `/support-incident-update` | Customer updates during an outage |

## Artifact Flow

```
.singularskills/support/acme/
  01-map.md              ← Channels, journey moments, contact reasons
  02-taxonomy.md         ← Categories, tags, priorities
  03-knowledge.md        ← Help centre plan and draft articles
  04-macros.md           ← Voice, policies, saved replies
  05-escalation.md       ← Tiers, triggers, handoffs
  06-service-levels.md   ← Targets, clock, staffing
  07-health.md           ← Metrics, SLA attainment, at-risk accounts
  qa/                    ← QA reviews
  incidents/             ← Outage updates
```

## Data You Bring

The skills work from ticket exports (CSV or JSON) and conversations you paste. They make no network calls and connect to no helpdesk. Without an export, a skill proceeds from what you describe and marks its measurements `not measured`.

## Cross-Domain Integration

- **Research** and **Marketing** artifacts inform the Map phase.
- **Branding**'s `03-verbal.md` sets the voice of every macro and reply.
- Suspected security incidents go to `/security-incident-response`; security incident wording belongs to `/security-incident-communication`.
- When the Health phase finds accounts at risk, `/marketing-churn-prevention` designs the interventions.
