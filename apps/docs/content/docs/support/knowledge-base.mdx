## Purpose

The Knowledge phase plans a help centre that lets customers answer their own questions, ranked by the contacts each article would prevent.

## Usage

**Example:**
```
/support-knowledge-base acme
```

## Prerequisites

Requires `.singularskills/support/<project>/02-taxonomy.md`. Use `--skip-checks` to bypass.

## What It Does

<Steps>
<Step>Reviews the current help centre</Step>
<Step>Designs sections that follow the taxonomy in the customer's words</Step>
<Step>Ranks an article backlog by deflectable volume</Step>
<Step>Writes the article template and the rule for turning tickets into articles</Step>
<Step>Drafts the top three articles in full</Step>
<Step>Writes the plan to `.singularskills/support/<project>/03-knowledge.md`</Step>
</Steps>

## Output

<Output>
Creates `.singularskills/support/<project>/03-knowledge.md` containing:

- Current state
- Structure
- Article backlog
- Article template
- Maintenance
- Draft articles
</Output>
