## Purpose

The Map phase shows who contacts support, through which channels, at which moments in their journey and about what, so every later decision starts from the real contact pattern.

## Usage

**Example:**
```
/support-journey-map acme
```

## Prerequisites

None — this is the first phase.

## What It Does

<Steps>
<Step>Reads research and marketing artifacts for the project, if any</Step>
<Step>Asks once for a ticket export and the active-customer count</Step>
<Step>Lists channels, who answers them and their hours</Step>
<Step>Maps the journey moments that trigger contact and the reasons at each</Step>
<Step>Computes current volumes from the export with a script, or marks them `not measured`</Step>
<Step>Writes the map to `.singularskills/support/<project>/01-map.md`</Step>
</Steps>

## Output

<Output>
Creates `.singularskills/support/<project>/01-map.md` containing:

- Channels
- Journey moments
- Contact reasons
- Current volumes (`computed` or `not measured`)
- Pain points
- Key takeaways
</Output>
