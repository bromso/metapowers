## Purpose

The Service levels phase sets how fast support answers, checks the team can keep up, and separates what customers are promised from what the team aims for.

## Usage

**Example:**
```
/support-service-levels acme
```

## Prerequisites

Requires `.singularskills/support/<project>/02-taxonomy.md` and `05-escalation.md`. Use `--skip-checks` to bypass.

## What It Does

<Steps>
<Step>Sets first-response and resolution targets per priority and channel</Step>
<Step>Defines business hours and what pauses the clock</Step>
<Step>Estimates the staffing the targets imply, computed with a script</Step>
<Step>Separates published promises from internal targets</Step>
<Step>Writes the service levels to `.singularskills/support/<project>/06-service-levels.md`</Step>
</Steps>

## Output

<Output>
Creates `.singularskills/support/<project>/06-service-levels.md` containing:

- Targets
- Clock
- Staffing estimate
- Published promises
- Internal targets
</Output>
