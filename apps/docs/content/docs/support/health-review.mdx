## Purpose

The Health phase measures how support is performing against its service levels and finds the accounts whose contacts say they may be about to leave.

## Usage

**Example:**
```
/support-health-review acme
```

## Prerequisites

Requires `.singularskills/support/<project>/02-taxonomy.md` and `06-service-levels.md`. Use `--skip-checks` to bypass.

## What It Does

<Steps>
<Step>Computes the metrics in `support-metrics.md` from the export, each `computed` or `not measured`</Step>
<Step>Checks SLA attainment per priority</Step>
<Step>Finds the categories driving volume</Step>
<Step>Lists at-risk accounts by a stable `acct-` identifier, never by personal data</Step>
<Step>Compares with the previous review on a re-run</Step>
<Step>Writes the review to `.singularskills/support/<project>/07-health.md`</Step>
</Steps>

## Output

<Output>
Creates `.singularskills/support/<project>/07-health.md` containing:

- Metrics
- SLA attainment
- Volume drivers
- At-risk accounts
- Trend
</Output>

## Next Step

When the review finds accounts at risk, run `/marketing-churn-prevention` to design the interventions.
