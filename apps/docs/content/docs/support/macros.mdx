## Purpose

The Responses phase writes the saved replies (macros) for the most frequent contact reasons and records the support policy they apply.

## Usage

**Example:**
```
/support-macros acme
```

## Prerequisites

Requires `.singularskills/support/<project>/02-taxonomy.md` and `03-knowledge.md`. Use `--skip-checks` to bypass.

## What It Does

<Steps>
<Step>Follows the brand voice from `branding/<project>/03-verbal.md`, or confirms one</Step>
<Step>Records the policy: may offer, needs approval, never promised</Step>
<Step>Writes each macro with its trigger, placeholders, linked article and pre-send checks</Step>
<Step>Applies the service-recovery checklist to every failure macro</Step>
<Step>Writes the macros to `.singularskills/support/<project>/04-macros.md`</Step>
</Steps>

## Output

<Output>
Creates `.singularskills/support/<project>/04-macros.md` containing:

- Voice
- Policies
- Macros
</Output>
