## Purpose

The Sustain phase establishes the recurring rituals and processes that keep your team healthy, aligned, and growing over time.

## Prerequisites

Requires `.singularskills/leadership/<team>/04-develop.md`. Use `--skip-checks` to bypass.

## What It Does

<Steps>
<Step>Defines recurring rituals (weekly 1:1s, biweekly retros, monthly reviews, quarterly OKRs)</Step>
<Step>Creates a leader's playbook for common situations</Step>
<Step>Establishes team health metrics and warning signs</Step>
<Step>Defines renewal triggers — when to revisit earlier phases</Step>
<Step>Writes to `.singularskills/leadership/<team>/05-sustain.md`</Step>
</Steps>

## Output

<Output>
Creates `.singularskills/leadership/<team>/05-sustain.md` containing ritual calendar, leader's playbook, health metrics, and renewal triggers.
</Output>

## After Phases

Use the [day-to-day tools](/docs/leadership#day-to-day-tools) for ongoing leadership work.
