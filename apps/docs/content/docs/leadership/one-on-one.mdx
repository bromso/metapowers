## Purpose

Prepare a structured 1:1 meeting agenda with talking points, feedback to deliver, and action items to follow up on.

## Usage

**Example:**
```
/leadership-one-on-one weekly with Sarah
```

## What It Does

<Steps>
<Step>Prepares an agenda following the 1:1 template (check-in, their topics, progress, growth, actions)</Step>
<Step>Suggests conversation starters based on context</Step>
<Step>Identifies feedback to deliver and growth goals to check in on</Step>
<Step>Writes to `.singularskills/leadership/<topic>/one-on-one.md`</Step>
</Steps>
