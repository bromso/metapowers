## Purpose

Create or review OKRs — clear objectives with measurable key results aligned to team and organizational goals.

## Usage

**Example:**
```
/leadership-okrs Q3 engineering team
```

## What It Does

<Steps>
<Step>Defines 2-3 objectives (qualitative, inspirational, time-bound)</Step>
<Step>Sets 3-5 measurable key results per objective (outcomes, not activities)</Step>
<Step>Aligns upward to organizational goals</Step>
<Step>Validates capacity and identifies what to deprioritize</Step>
<Step>Writes to `.singularskills/leadership/<topic>/okrs.md`</Step>
</Steps>
