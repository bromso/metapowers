## Purpose

The Build phase structures your team for clarity and autonomy — who owns what, who decides what, and how work flows between people.

## Prerequisites

Requires `.singularskills/leadership/<team>/02-vision.md`. Use `--skip-checks` to bypass.

## What It Does

<Steps>
<Step>Maps each team member's role and primary responsibilities</Step>
<Step>Defines ownership areas and decision rights (RAPID framework)</Step>
<Step>Creates a delegation framework matched to member maturity</Step>
<Step>Establishes collaboration norms and escalation paths</Step>
<Step>Writes to `.singularskills/leadership/<team>/03-build.md`</Step>
</Steps>

## Output

<Output>
Creates `.singularskills/leadership/<team>/03-build.md` containing team structure, decision rights, delegation framework, and collaboration norms.
</Output>
