## Purpose

The Develop phase focuses on growing your people — setting team OKRs, creating individual development plans, and establishing a feedback cadence.

## Prerequisites

Requires `.metapowers/leadership/<team>/03-build.md`. Use `--skip-checks` to bypass.

## What It Does

<Steps>
<Step>Sets 2-3 team-level OKRs with measurable key results</Step>
<Step>Creates individual growth plans per team member</Step>
<Step>Defines the feedback cadence (weekly 1:1s, monthly reviews, quarterly OKRs)</Step>
<Step>Documents your support commitments</Step>
<Step>Writes to `.metapowers/leadership/<team>/04-develop.md`</Step>
</Steps>

## Output

<Output>
Creates `.metapowers/leadership/<team>/04-develop.md` containing team OKRs, individual growth plans, feedback cadence, and support commitments.
</Output>
