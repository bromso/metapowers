## Purpose

The Assess phase evaluates your leadership landscape — your style, your team's maturity levels, and the key challenges to address. This foundation informs all subsequent phases.

## Usage

**Example:**
```
/leadership:assess engineering-team
```

## What It Does

<Steps>
<Step>Asks questions about your role, team size, experience levels, and challenges</Step>
<Step>Assesses each team member's maturity and autonomy needs</Step>
<Step>Identifies your natural leadership style and potential blind spots</Step>
<Step>Prioritizes challenges to address</Step>
<Step>Writes the assessment to `.metapowers/leadership/<team>/01-assess.md`</Step>
</Steps>

## Output

<Output>
Creates `.metapowers/leadership/<team>/01-assess.md` containing:

- Leadership profile (style, strengths, blind spots)
- Team member maturity assessment
- Prioritized challenges
- Recommended focus areas
</Output>
