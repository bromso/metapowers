## Purpose

The Vision phase defines what your team stands for — its purpose, values, and cultural norms. This becomes the foundation for every leadership decision.

## Prerequisites

Requires `.metapowers/leadership/<team>/01-assess.md` from the Assess phase. Use `--skip-checks` to bypass.

## What It Does

<Steps>
<Step>Defines team purpose and connection to organizational mission</Step>
<Step>Establishes 3-5 core values with behavioral examples</Step>
<Step>Creates a culture blueprint (decision-making, communication, conflict norms)</Step>
<Step>Paints a success vision for 6 months out</Step>
<Step>Writes to `.metapowers/leadership/<team>/02-vision.md`</Step>
</Steps>

## Output

<Output>
Creates `.metapowers/leadership/<team>/02-vision.md` containing team purpose, values, culture blueprint, and success vision.
</Output>
