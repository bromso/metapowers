## Purpose

The Tax Prep utility organizes financial records into the categories and formats required for tax filing, ensuring completeness and identifying potential deductions or issues before submission.

## What It Does

<Steps>
<Step>Reviews categorized transactions for tax-relevant classifications</Step>
<Step>Groups deductible expenses by tax category</Step>
<Step>Identifies missing documentation or receipts</Step>
<Step>Summarizes revenue, expenses, and estimated tax liability</Step>
<Step>Flags items requiring accountant review</Step>
</Steps>

## Output

<Output>
- Tax category summary with totals
- Missing documentation checklist
- Estimated tax liability overview
- Items flagged for professional review
</Output>
