## Purpose

The Accounts Receivable utility tracks incoming payments owed to the organization — monitoring outstanding invoices, aging balances, and collection activities.

## What It Does

<Steps>
<Step>Logs customer invoices with amounts, due dates, and terms</Step>
<Step>Tracks collection status (outstanding, partial, paid, written off)</Step>
<Step>Generates aging reports for outstanding receivables</Step>
<Step>Identifies overdue accounts for follow-up</Step>
<Step>Records payment receipts and applies them to invoices</Step>
</Steps>

## Output

<Output>
- Receivables register with collection status
- Aging report (current, 30, 60, 90+ days)
- Collection priority list
</Output>
