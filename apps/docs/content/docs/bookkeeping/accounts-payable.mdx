## Purpose

The Accounts Payable utility manages outgoing payments — tracking invoices from vendors, scheduling payments based on due dates and cash flow, and maintaining a clear record of outstanding obligations.

## What It Does

<Steps>
<Step>Logs vendor invoices with amounts, due dates, and payment terms</Step>
<Step>Tracks payment status (pending, scheduled, paid, overdue)</Step>
<Step>Prioritizes payments by due date and discount opportunities</Step>
<Step>Generates an aging report for outstanding payables</Step>
<Step>Records payment confirmations</Step>
</Steps>

## Output

<Output>
- Invoice register with payment status
- Aging report (current, 30, 60, 90+ days)
- Upcoming payment schedule
</Output>
