## Purpose

The Chart of Accounts utility designs and maintains the hierarchical account structure used to categorize all financial transactions. A well-structured chart of accounts is the foundation of accurate bookkeeping.

## What It Does

<Steps>
<Step>Designs an account hierarchy tailored to the business type</Step>
<Step>Assigns account numbers following standard numbering conventions</Step>
<Step>Groups accounts into categories (assets, liabilities, equity, revenue, expenses)</Step>
<Step>Defines sub-accounts for granular tracking</Step>
<Step>Validates the structure for completeness and consistency</Step>
</Steps>

## Output

<Output>
- Complete chart of accounts with account numbers and descriptions
- Category hierarchy diagram
- Account usage guidelines
</Output>
