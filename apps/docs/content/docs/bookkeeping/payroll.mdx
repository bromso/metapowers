## Purpose

The Payroll utility manages employee compensation records — tracking wages, withholdings, deductions, and employer contributions for accurate payroll bookkeeping.

## What It Does

<Steps>
<Step>Records gross wages by employee and pay period</Step>
<Step>Calculates withholdings (income tax, social security, medicare)</Step>
<Step>Tracks voluntary deductions (benefits, retirement contributions)</Step>
<Step>Records employer-side payroll taxes and contributions</Step>
<Step>Produces payroll summaries by period</Step>
</Steps>

## Output

<Output>
- Payroll register by pay period
- Deduction and withholding summary
- Employer contribution totals
</Output>
