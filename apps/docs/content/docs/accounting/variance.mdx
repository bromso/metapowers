## Purpose

The Variance utility compares actual financial results against budgeted or expected amounts, identifying favorable and unfavorable variances and their root causes.

## What It Does

<Steps>
<Step>Reads actual results from the financial statements</Step>
<Step>Compares against budget or prior period data</Step>
<Step>Calculates dollar and percentage variances by account</Step>
<Step>Classifies variances as favorable or unfavorable</Step>
<Step>Identifies significant variances requiring investigation</Step>
</Steps>

## Output

<Output>
- Variance report by account with dollar and percentage differences
- Favorable/unfavorable classification
- Significant variance highlights with suggested root causes
</Output>
