## Purpose

The Ledger phase transfers journal entries to their respective accounts in the general ledger, accumulating balances and providing a complete record of activity for each account.

## Prerequisites

Journal entries must exist at `.singularskills/accounting/<project>/02-journal.md`.

## What It Does

<Steps>
<Step>Reads journal entries from the Journal phase</Step>
<Step>Posts each debit and credit to the corresponding ledger account</Step>
<Step>Calculates running balances for each account</Step>
<Step>Cross-references entries back to journal entry numbers</Step>
<Step>Writes the general ledger to `.singularskills/accounting/<project>/03-ledger.md`</Step>
</Steps>

## Output

<Output>
Creates `.singularskills/accounting/<project>/03-ledger.md` containing:

- Individual account ledgers with running balances
- Journal entry cross-references
- Account balance summary
</Output>
