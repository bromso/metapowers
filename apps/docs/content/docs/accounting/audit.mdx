## Purpose

The Audit utility performs detailed reviews of specific accounts or transaction classes, applying substantive testing procedures to verify accuracy, completeness, and validity of recorded amounts.

## What It Does

<Steps>
<Step>Selects accounts or transaction classes for review</Step>
<Step>Designs audit procedures (vouching, tracing, analytical review)</Step>
<Step>Tests sample transactions against source documents</Step>
<Step>Evaluates account balances for reasonableness</Step>
<Step>Documents findings and exceptions</Step>
</Steps>

## Output

<Output>
- Audit program with procedures and scope
- Test results with exceptions noted
- Findings summary with recommendations
</Output>
