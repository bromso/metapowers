## Purpose

The Close phase zeroes out temporary accounts (revenue, expense, dividends) by transferring their balances to retained earnings, resetting them for the next accounting period.

## Prerequisites

Financial statements must exist at `.singularskills/accounting/<project>/07-statements.md`.

## What It Does

<Steps>
<Step>Identifies all temporary accounts (revenue, expense, dividends)</Step>
<Step>Creates closing entries to transfer balances to retained earnings</Step>
<Step>Verifies all temporary accounts have zero balances after closing</Step>
<Step>Produces a post-closing trial balance</Step>
<Step>Writes closing entries to `.singularskills/accounting/<project>/08-close.md`</Step>
</Steps>

## Output

<Output>
Creates `.singularskills/accounting/<project>/08-close.md` containing:

- Closing journal entries
- Post-closing trial balance (permanent accounts only)
- Confirmation that temporary accounts are zeroed
</Output>
