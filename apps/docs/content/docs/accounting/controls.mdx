## Purpose

The Controls utility designs internal controls — segregation of duties, approval workflows, access restrictions, and reconciliation checkpoints — to safeguard assets and ensure accuracy of financial records.

## What It Does

<Steps>
<Step>Identifies key financial processes and risk points</Step>
<Step>Designs preventive and detective controls for each process</Step>
<Step>Defines approval authority levels and thresholds</Step>
<Step>Establishes segregation of duties requirements</Step>
<Step>Documents control procedures and responsibilities</Step>
</Steps>

## Output

<Output>
- Control matrix mapping risks to control activities
- Approval workflow diagrams with authority levels
- Segregation of duties assignments
</Output>
