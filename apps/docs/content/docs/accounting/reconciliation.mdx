## Purpose

The Reconciliation utility verifies that related accounts agree with each other — such as sub-ledger to general ledger reconciliation, intercompany balances, or control account verification.

## What It Does

<Steps>
<Step>Identifies account pairs or groups requiring reconciliation</Step>
<Step>Extracts balances from both sides</Step>
<Step>Matches individual items and identifies differences</Step>
<Step>Investigates and classifies reconciling items</Step>
<Step>Documents resolution actions for outstanding items</Step>
</Steps>

## Output

<Output>
- Reconciliation worksheets for each account pair
- Reconciling items with classifications (timing, errors, omissions)
- Action items for unresolved differences
</Output>
