## Purpose

The Statements phase prepares the three core financial statements from the adjusted trial balance — the income statement, balance sheet, and statement of cash flows.

## Prerequisites

The adjusted trial balance must exist at `.singularskills/accounting/<project>/06-adjusted-balance.md`.

## What It Does

<Steps>
<Step>Reads the adjusted trial balance</Step>
<Step>Separates revenue and expense accounts for the income statement</Step>
<Step>Calculates net income and transfers to retained earnings</Step>
<Step>Organizes asset, liability, and equity accounts for the balance sheet</Step>
<Step>Derives cash flows from operating, investing, and financing activities</Step>
<Step>Writes financial statements to `.singularskills/accounting/<project>/07-statements.md`</Step>
</Steps>

## Output

<Output>
Creates `.singularskills/accounting/<project>/07-statements.md` containing:

- Income statement (revenue, expenses, net income)
- Balance sheet (assets, liabilities, equity)
- Statement of cash flows (operating, investing, financing)
</Output>
